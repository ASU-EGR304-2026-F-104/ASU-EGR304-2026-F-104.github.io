    Need Feature Specifics
    1. Drowsiness Detection a. Camera
    b. Yawn detection
    c. Infrared camera
    d. Pressure sensor
    e. Motion sensor
    f. Eye tracking
    g. Head position sensor
    h. Steering wheel sensor
    i. Arm detection
    j. Camera outside car    		 a. Detects signs of fatigue on driver’s face
    b. Detects consistent yawning
    c. Allows monitoring driver’s face in lowlight conditions
    d. Compares if there's any change in pressure on the steering wheel
    e. Detects if driver isn’t moving regularly
    f. Detects if driver’s eyes are closed for a long time
    g. Detects the driver’s initial head position to see if it’s changed
    h. Detects if the steering wheel is being moved unusually
    i. Detects in driver’s arms let go of the steering wheel
    j. Detects if vehicle is swerving outside the lane and alert the driver
    2. Comfort a. Soft material for adjustable strap
    b. Lightweight
    c. Adjustable volume
    d. Adjustable vibration
    e. Alert mode options
    f. Wireless device
    g. Smooth finish
    h. Breathable material
    i. Soft padding
    j. Moldable material    		 a. Lets the device be adjustable to the driver’s fit and comfort
    b. Lets the driver focus on the road instead of feeling the weight of the device
    c. Alerts the driver with audio alert they can set on their personal preference
    d. Lets the driver choose a setting to wake them up but doesn’t hurt them
    e. Let the driver choose what type of alert is most effective for them between noise, vibration, flashing LED, etc.
    f. Allows the driver to use and move the device comfortably, minimizes distractions
    g. Avoids sharp edges to avoid injury and discomfort while in use
    h. If adjustable, make material lightweight and breathable to avoid sweating
    i. Adds padding between device and drivers skin
    j. Allows driver to fit device comfortably where they place it on themselves
    3. Ease of Use a. One button to function
    b. Generic options
    c. Automatic set up
    d. Clear labels
    e. User manual
    f. Alert levels
    g. Volume dial
    h. LED brightness level
    i. Pre set vibration setting
    j. Foldable    		 a. Lets the driver turn on and start the device with one button
    b. Avoid overwhelming the driver with too many options
    c. Device begins to function when driver is beginning their commute
    d. Lets driver easily identify the features
    e. Provides instructions for set up and operation
    f. Lets the driver choose between high or low alert intensity
    g. Lets the driver choose audio alert setting easily
    h. Lets driver choose intensity of brightness between extreme and high brightness
    i. Lets driver choose intensity of vibration alert system between high or extreme
    j. Lets driver put away and take out to use easily with minimal setup
    4. Effective Alerts a. Audible alert
    b. Vibration alert
    c. LED flashing alert
    d. Pre recorded audio alert
    e. Repeated alert
    f. Combination alerts setting
    g. Alert intensity depending on use
    h. Timed check in with prerecorded message
    i. Adjustable audio alert
    j. Adjustable vibration    		 a. Will alert the driver with a loud alarm, buzzer, etc.
    b. Will alert the driver with physical vibration if the driver is very drowsy
    c. Will alert the driver with flashing LEDs to get their attention
    d. Will play pre recorded messages/warnings to alert the driver
    e. Will continue to alert the driver until they deactivate the alarm
    f. Will alert the driver by combining audio, LED, and vibration all at once
    g. Device will make alert more intense the longer the device is worn and activated
    h. Have the device at specific times alert and the driver must press a button to stop the alarm
    i. Driver can change audio loudness
    j. Driver can change vibration intensity
    5. Durability a. Water resistance
    b. Non-brittle and rigid shell
    c. Protected battery
    d. Safe against accidental drops
    e. Temperature resistance
    f. Button durability
    g. UV-resistance
    h. Easy-to-clean materials
    i. Storage durability
    j. Long-term reliability    		 a. Able to withstand spills or sweat without damaging components.
    b. The device should be able to slightly flex without deforming.
    c. Padding around the battery to protect damage from drops.
    d. Design should be durable enough to protect internal components.
    e. Protection that allows electrical components to withstand hot temperatures.
    f. Buttons should be designed in a way to not break from drops or scratches.
    g. UV resistance prevents the outer housing from becoming brittle over time.
    h. The design should be easy to clean and repair.
    i. A durable shell shouldn’t deform from storage in a user’s backpack or purse
    j. User-replaceable parts promote long-term reliability.
    6. Customization a. Adjustable vibration intensity
    b. Adjustable LED lighting
    c. Adjustable volume
    d. Alert frequency
    e. Personalized presets
    f. Neckband size adjustment
    g. Variable product colors
    h. Customizable
    i. Re-programmable button layout
    j. Opensourced code    		 a. The user may adjust the intensity of the vibration alert for light or heavy sleepers.
    b. The user may adjust the intensity of the LED light for epilepsy.
    c. The user may adjust the intensity of the volume to comfort.
    d. Users may adjust the frequency of the alerts when the device begins to detect drowsiness.
    e. Presets allow users to have different alert intensities for the time of day.
    f. Different neckband sizes accommodate different physical neck shapes.
    g. Users may select from a variety of product colors that suit their personality or style.
    h. The product’s external shell may be customized.
    i. Users may re-program each button to do a function other than drowsy detection.
    j. Opensourcing the code allows users to unlock the full customization of their product.
    7. Quality a. High durability
    b. Longevity
    c. Heat resistant
    d. Long battery life
    e. Comfortable to use
    f. Servicable
    g. Ease of use
    h. Aesthetic
    i. Good material
    j. Works    		 a. Can withstand falls or accidental crushes
    b. Device functions for a long time before replacement is needed
    c. Can withstand hot car temperatures, 120°F+
    d. Battery lasts long enough to stay on all night
    e. Driver can use it all night without discomfort
    f. Driver can make simple fixes to the device
    g. Device is easy to figure out
    h. Device is pleasing to look at and handle
    i. Made with high quality materials
    j. The product works as intended
    8. Safety a. Noticeable alerts
    b. Responsive
    c. Safe volumes
    d. Crash compliant
    e. Includes instructions
    f. Failsafes
    g. Multiple sensors
    h. 911 connection
    i. Timed check-ins
    j. Multiple alerts    		 a. Alerts should be loud/strong enough to be noticed by a drowsy driver
    b. Device should have a quick response time
    c. The device is not too loud as to scare or damage the driver
    d. The device won’t be a projectile in a crash
    e. Device comes with a manual to review safety instructions
    f. The device has many ways to function so if one fails it still works
    g. There are multiple ways to detect drowsiness on a driver
    h. The device can alert 911 if there was a crash
    i. The device can set timed alarms to keep the driver awake between head jerks
    j. The device can use sound and vibration and lights to keep the driver awake
    9. Reliability a. Multiple sensors
    b. Different methods of alerting user
    c. Low battery notifications
    d. Auto restart if chip freezes
    e. Waterproof materials
    f. Redundant processing
    g. Different types of sensors
    h. under/over voltage protection
    i. Replaceable parts
    j. Fixable design    		 a. Multiple sensors for redundant info
    b. Multiple methods of alerting drivers
    c. Alert the driver of a low battery so they are aware it could die and not to rely on device
    d. If the processor freezes or hangs auto restart the chip
    e. Water or moisture can be a problem in a variety of situations
    f. Multiple processors that can reduce errors or provide redundancy
    g. Multiple types of sensors for redundant data
    h. Circuit protection for under or over voltage and providing constant power
    i. Good for repairability and maintenance
    j. Easy to access repairability for consumers
    10. Performance for Different Conditions a. Extreme temperature resistance
    b. Vibration dampening
    c. Low light effectiveness
    d. High humidity/wet environment
    e. Bright lights
    f. altitude/pressure tolerance
    g. Sound filtering
    h. dust/debris resistance
    i. No need for gps/signal
    j. Different skin contact    		 a. Cars get very hot and cold so preventing temperature affecting performance is important
    b. Cars move and shake and so do people so it sensor data should be resistant to vibration interference
    c. People tend to be sleepiest at night so the sensors need to work during night or low light conditions
    d. People live in humid or wet environments so sensors need to work despite moisture or water interference
    e. The sun may interfere with light based sensors so
    f. Atmospheric pressure can alter sensor data so the product needs to account for that
    g. Roads are loud environments so sensor and alert effectiveness needs to take background noise into account
    h. The device can get dusty or dirty due to usage or environment and needs to work in sub-optimal environments
    i. The product should either not include outside signals or be functional in the absence of them
    j. People’s skin can different humidities, capacitance, and texture that can affect sensor data.

    Sorting, Ranking, Grouping

    Sort your features / ideas thematically, by function, by the user need they satisfy, or some other logical grouping. Perhaps one group includes strategies for keeping attention; maybe another group includes user interface ideas. Regardless, group them along the different themes that emerge from the brainstorm. Spend some time ranking and discussing top ideas. Indicate which features rose to the top of each group. Generate new features from the ideas that came out of your discussion Capture and save your brainstorm at this point before you start to collect them together
