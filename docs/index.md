## Team Introduction
> <br>
>Our team is designing a device that is made up of four subsystems that will all be connected to work together. Its goal is to detect fatigue or drowsiness for drivers behind the wheel, and improve driver safety. It will detect head movement, process signals, and produce outputs. This system includes a photoresistor, gyroscope, speaker, and motor that are managed by a PIC microcontroller. .<br>
