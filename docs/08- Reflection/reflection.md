## Recommendations for Future Students
<br> 1. _Plan accordingly_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;Throughout this course, labs and assignments will need time outside of class to work on them. Get in the habit of being disciplined on time management and planning ahead. **Do not leave anything for the day its due expecting to complete the assignment**. <br> 2. _Ask Questions_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;Throughout this course, there will be many new concepts and applications used. Become used to communicating with ta's and professors and ask questions if you're unsure of something. <br> 3. _Communication_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;Throughout this course, there will be a team project. Get into the habit of communicating often with updates, deciding on when to meet, and making decisions as a team. Also communicate with the teachers if any questions during office hours or through email. <br> 4. _Schematics_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;Throughout this course, many labs include schematics that need to be built on a breadboard for chec offs. Practice interpreting and reading schematics to make sure connections are correct when building. <br> 5. _Soldering_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;For the team project, you will need to solder your own PCB Board. Practice soldering and make time as it can be vry time consuming.
