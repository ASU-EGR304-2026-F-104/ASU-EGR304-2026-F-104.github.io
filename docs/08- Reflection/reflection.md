## Lessons Learned
<br> In this course we learned many things. The top 10 most important things we learned as a team from this project would be: communication, time management, planning ahead, interpreting schematics, constructing a block diagram, soldering, making footprints, designing a PCB, creating gerber files, ordering and printing a PCB. Throughout this project, our team was in constant communication. We learned to collaborate with each other and created a safe space to express our opinions. Our team had to consistently keep in mind deadlines and plan accordingly to meet outside of class time to complete the tasks. We had to plan ahead and get group assignments done in a timely manner to not delay the progress of the team as a whole. We created and interpreted schematics during the labs and in KiCad for our own subsystems. The lab schematics were very helpful in practicing how to read and interpret them. Making a block diagram was very helpful in keeping our project organized and having a reference. Soldering was very important in this project. We had to solder our subsystem and make sure it was done well to prevent any bad connection. Making footprints through KiCad was a big challenge. It was important to double check measurements as we had issues with footprints after the boards arrived. With the practice we had with the schematics and footprints, we were able to design our subsystem PCB a bit more comfortably. Creating a gerber file was something new that we hadn't done, but thankfully wasn't too hard following the instructions given to download the files. One of our biggest point of confusion was the process to order the PCB to print. We had to ask and find out how to succesfully send an order form to get the boards ordered.

## Recommendations for Future Students
<br> 1. _Plan accordingly_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;Throughout this course, labs and assignments will need time outside of class to work on them. Get in the habit of being disciplined on time management and planning ahead. **Do not leave anything for the day its due expecting to complete the assignment**. <br> 2. _Ask Questions_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;Throughout this course, there will be many new concepts and applications used. Become used to communicating with ta's and professors and ask questions if you're unsure of something. <br> 3. _Communication_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;Throughout this course, there will be a team project. Get into the habit of communicating often with updates, deciding on when to meet, and making decisions as a team. Also communicate with the teachers if any questions during office hours or through email. <br> 4. _Schematics_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;Throughout this course, many labs include schematics that need to be built on a breadboard for check offs. Practice interpreting and reading schematics to make sure connections are correct when building. <br> 5. _Soldering_ <br> &emsp;&emsp;For the team project, you will need to solder your own PCB Board. Practice soldering and make time as it can be vry time consuming.
