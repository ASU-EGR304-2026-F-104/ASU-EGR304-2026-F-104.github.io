## Team Charter



> HaMaLoYa aims to provide awareness and safety for drivers who may encounter drowsiness or fatigue while behind the wheel.

## Product Mission Statement



 > HaMaLoYa's mission is to improve driver safety by developing an effective solution that can respond to fatigue or drowsiness, as these situations could happen at any moment.
