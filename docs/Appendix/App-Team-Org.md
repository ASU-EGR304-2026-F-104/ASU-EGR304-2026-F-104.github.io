## Meeting Coordination

3. The preferred format for meetings is virtually through discord.

## Team Coordination and Accountability

The team will make sure the assignments are submitted before the due date and make sure each team member has ok’d before submitting, by having the project monitor ensure the work is completed 12 hours before the due date.
The team will  make sure each member has the knowledge and skill needed for each assignment, and  will we adjust by having transparent communication, and being open minded on feedback.
The team will make sure the feedback given from our design review is distributed and acted on by each team member during our weekly check ins to make sure each member is updated on everything.  

**Additional questions were asked between the team:**

1. How will the team address missed contributions/assignments/actions? The project monitor will text the person privately.
2. How will the team hold each other accountable to the expectations of the charter? As a team we can reflect on who did what after each project or assignment in order to figure out responsibility moving forward.
3. How will the team recognize that a team member is underperforming? Depending on the amount of work designated and quality of work to the member. We will do overall weekly check ins to see if anyone is struggling.
4. How will the team help an underperforming team member improve? As a team we can offer assistance and help the individual improve their team work and collaboration skills. 
5. What is the agreed upon consequence if an underperforming team member doesn’t improve? We will try to communicate first. If there is no improvement, we will move forward with the pink slip.


## Conflict Recognition & Resolution

The team handle disagreements during the weekly check ins to discuss any issues and try to solve it as a team. We will make sure everyone feels comfortable expressing their opinion by having constant open dialogue. We will prevent conflicts of interest, or if one person is taking on too many roles by assigning roles/duties as a team for every project. 

The steps we decided on to resolve conflicts will be:
1. Ask with an open dialogue about needed support
2. Reminder about assigned duties
3. Team assistance for one assignment
4. Contact the instructor

We decided that a problem that will get an instructor involved is if a team member has to take over another person's responsibility to submit the assignment on time more than once.
The current meeting leader or meeting recorder will contact the instructor if a conflict needs to be addressed by them.
Overall, the agreed upon process for resolving conflicts is through communication at weekly check ins with all members present.
