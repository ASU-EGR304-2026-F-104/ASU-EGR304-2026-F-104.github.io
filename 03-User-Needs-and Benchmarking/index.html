    Need Statement Process

    After compiling the statements into a list of 100 entries, the group went through all the user statements and separated them based on what question they answer:

    • Does the product actually wake you up?
    • Does the product work as intended?
    • Is the product good quality?
    • How long can the product last?
    • Is the product comfortable and convenient for the consumer?
    • How is the product perceived in the public eye?

    Then, as shown in Figure 3.6 below, six statements were selected to be the meta statements for each category.

    Figure 3.6 Selected meta statements

    The list of 100 statements was then divided up by which question it answered best and counted. The highest priorities for the product was decided based on the number of statements each category had.
